# Your website: how to run it

Everything you need is in the WordPress dashboard. Log in at **yoursite.com/wp-admin**.

On the left you'll see an **Estimate** menu. That's where almost everything lives.

---

## See new estimates

1. Click **Estimate → Estimates**.
2. New ones are marked **New** (the number next to "Estimate" in the menu tells you how many).
3. Click **Open** on a row to see everything the customer entered: their details, every answer grouped by project, and their photos. Click a photo to see it full size.
4. On the right, change **Status** to *Contacted*, *Proposal sent* or *Closed* and click **Save status**. This is just for you, to keep track.

Every estimate is saved here even if an email doesn't arrive, so nothing is ever lost.

## See contact messages

**Estimate → Messages** works the same way, for the contact form on the Contact page.

## Get estimates by email

1. **Estimate → Settings**.
2. Put your email in **Send new estimates and messages to**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Each new estimate is then emailed to you. Hitting *Reply* writes straight back to the customer.

> Ask your developer to set up the email sender (WP Mail SMTP) when the site goes live. Without it, emails can land in spam or never arrive. The estimates are still saved in the dashboard either way.

## Change the questions, options and photos

**Estimate → Builder.** This is the whole online estimate.

- **Left side:** the projects customers can choose (Bathroom, Windows…). Click one to edit it. ↑ ↓ change the order, ✕ deletes it, **+ Add project** adds a new one.
- **Pages:** each project is a list of pages, in the order customers see them. Click a page to open it.
- **Questions:** inside a page. Click a question to open it.
  - Change the wording in **Question**.
  - **Type:** *Pick one*, *Pick any* (checkboxes), a text box, or an *Information note*.
  - **Show options as:** text choices, photo cards, or small tiles.
  - **Customer must answer this:** tick it to make the question required.
- **Options:** change the text, click the little picture to choose a photo from your Media Library (or upload a new one), and use **+ Add option** to add more.
- **Show this question / Show this page:** rules like "only when *Design* is *Let me design it*". Pick the question, tick the answers. Leave it empty and it's always shown.

When you're done, click **Save changes** at the top. The live estimate updates straight away. Click **View the estimate ↗** to check it.

**Made a mistake?**
- **Discard changes** throws away what you haven't saved.
- **Reset to the original setup** puts everything back to how it was delivered.
- **Export** downloads a backup file of your setup. **Import** loads one back. It's a good idea to export before big changes.

## Change your business name, logo or phone number

**Estimate → Settings**, then **Save Changes**. The name and logo update across the whole site.

## Change the Privacy Policy

**Pages → Privacy Policy**, edit the text, then **Update**.

## Change page titles for Google

Each page has a **Yoast SEO** box under the editor (**Pages → choose a page**). Fill in the SEO title and meta description there.

## What you can't change here

The text and photos on Home, Windows, Bath Remodel, Our Work and About are part of the design. Send changes to your developer and they'll update them.
